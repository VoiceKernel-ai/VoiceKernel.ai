# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person
who wrote it.

## What that means here

Welcome: direct technical disagreement, strong opinions about design, saying
"this is wrong and here is why", asking questions that seem obvious.

Not welcome: personal attacks, harassment, demeaning comments about anyone's
experience level, and the kind of sustained dismissiveness that makes people
stop contributing.

Reviews should be about the change. "This breaks tenant isolation because X" is
useful. "Did you even read the code?" is not, and says nothing the first
sentence did not.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. Responses range from a private word to a permanent ban,
depending on severity and pattern.

Maintainers who do not follow this are subject to it too.
